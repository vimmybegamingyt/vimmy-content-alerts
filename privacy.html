Privacy Policy

Vimmy Content Alerts

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Overview

Vimmy Content Alerts is a creator-operated automation designed to detect newly published content from authorized social media accounts and notify the Vimmy community when new content is available.

TikTok Data

When the TikTok integration is authorized, Vimmy Content Alerts may access information made available through TikTok's authorized APIs, including basic account information and information about recent videos necessary to identify newly published content and generate links to those posts.

How Information Is Used

Information obtained through TikTok is used only to operate the content notification functionality of Vimmy Content Alerts. This includes identifying newly published videos, preventing duplicate notifications, and providing a link to the newly published content.

Data Sharing and Sale

Vimmy Content Alerts does not sell TikTok user data. Information obtained through TikTok is not provided to advertisers or data brokers.

Data Storage

The integration may locally retain limited information necessary for operation, such as the identifier of the most recently processed post, so that duplicate notifications can be prevented.

Access and Authorization

TikTok access is authorized by the TikTok account owner. Access may be revoked through the applicable TikTok account or authorization controls.

Third-Party Services

Vimmy Content Alerts may interact with third-party services such as TikTok and Discord to retrieve authorized content information and deliver content notifications. Use of those services is also subject to their respective policies and terms.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the functionality of Vimmy Content Alerts changes or when necessary to reflect applicable platform requirements.
