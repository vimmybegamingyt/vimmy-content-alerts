Vimmy Content Alerts

Vimmy Content Alerts is a creator-operated desktop automation used to monitor authorized social media accounts for newly published content.

When new content is detected, the application retrieves the information necessary to identify the post and sends a notification containing a link to Vimmy's Discord community.

TikTok Integration

TikTok access is authorized by the account owner. The integration is used to retrieve information about the authorized account's recent videos for the purpose of detecting newly published content.

Vimmy Content Alerts does not publish TikTok content, modify TikTok accounts, sell TikTok data, or provide TikTok account information to advertisers.
