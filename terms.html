About Vimmy Content Alerts

Vimmy Content Alerts is a creator-operated automation designed to detect newly published content from authorized social media accounts and provide notifications containing links to that content.

Authorized Use

Access to supported social media services must be authorized through the applicable platform's official authorization process. Vimmy Content Alerts is intended to access only information permitted by the account owner and the applicable platform.

TikTok Integration

When authorized by the TikTok account owner, Vimmy Content Alerts may use TikTok's APIs to retrieve permitted account and recent-video information for the purpose of detecting newly published content and generating links to those posts.

Content Notifications

Notifications generated by Vimmy Content Alerts may be delivered to community services such as Discord. The automation may retain limited information, such as the identifier of the most recently processed post, to prevent duplicate notifications.

TikTok, Discord, and other third-party services used with Vimmy Content Alerts operate under their own terms, policies, availability, and technical requirements. Vimmy Content Alerts is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by those third-party platforms.

Availability
